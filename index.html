Private-use · Self-hosted

An assistant for everyday organization.

Orivance Personal Assistant is software for organizing its owner's personal communications, reminders, and household follow-ups. It is not offered as a public sign-up service.

Optional read-only Gmail connection

When separately authorized by the Gmail account owner, the assistant reads newly received messages to help identify important correspondence, bills, receipts, renewals, and follow-ups.

Its Gmail integration requests only gmail.readonly. This connection does not send, delete, archive, mark read, or modify email, and it does not authorize purchases or bill payment.

Owner control and privacy

Email processing and storage occur in the owner's private, self-hosted environment. This public informational website has no inbox, login, OAuth callback, or email database.

Other functions, such as summaries sent through a separately configured messaging channel, require independent authorization.

Learn more in the Privacy Policy.

This site exists to describe the application for its Google OAuth consent configuration. Its operational services, code, account credentials, and stored data are not published here.
